02 / Quick workflow

Work from dialogue to music, with a speech rebuild as an optional last pass. Check off each step as you go; this checklist belongs to the guide only.

03 / QAQC

These tools help you decide where to review. Neither one fixes audio or replaces an audition.

Full audible sequence · no selection needed

Verify loudness

Measures the current mix, including mute/solo and effects. Reports integrated LUFS and sample peaks; optionally adds markers at peaks over your flag level.

  • Default target: −24 LUFS, tolerance ±1 LU (Broadcast). For YouTube choose Delivery → YouTube (−14 LUFS, peaks over −2 dBFS flagged), or use the YouTube preset.
  • Default peak flag: over −6 dBFS; hits within 2 seconds merge.
  • Set the actual delivery target in Options → Loudness verification. This does not change gain.
Sample peak is not true peak. A reading within the target is not a delivery compliance certificate.
Selected dialogue clips · connection required

Check dialogue quality

Scores each selected audio clip independently and adds red review markers. Unselected clips, including those on the same track, are excluded.

1.32

Example marker title. The description shows “overall 1.32, speech 1.48, background 1.91. (Lower is worse)”.

  • Flag below defaults to 1.5; higher values flag more audio.
  • Scores describe roughly nine-second review windows, not exact defect boundaries.
  • Whispers, music and unusual voices can score poorly. Listen before changing anything.
Listen for: complete words, natural voice character, clean clip joins, retained breaths, steady levels and music that returns smoothly. Recheck the audible mix after Rebuild Speech or other final changes.

04 / Extras

These are separate actions. You do not need to run them on every clip.

Targeted repair

Fix pop at playhead

Select one audio clip, park the playhead on the pop, then click Fix pop at playhead. Listen through the repair and the surrounding syllables. Runs in the plugin; no connection needed.

Background separation

Extract ambience

Select the source clips and click Extract ambience. Adds an Ambience track while leaving the originals in place. Requires connection. Balance the result by ear with the cleaned voice.

Steady background noise

Learn noise, then Remove noise

Mark In and Out on the sequence around 0.5–60 seconds of noise only (room tone, hum or hiss between lines), click Learn noise, then select the clips and click Remove noise. The results go on a Noise removed track. The noise print is kept with this project. Requires connection. Turn down Noise reduction in Options if voices sound thin.

Comparing versions? Select muted finds clips silenced by WTVAudioWizard. Unmute restores those originals. To keep originals audible from the start, untick Mute raw when done on the Main tab before processing. Mute the processed counterpart for a clean A/B comparison; avoid summing both versions.

05 / Settings reference

Batch Process Options are on the Main tab; everything else is under Options. Each group expands on demand. Changing a value does not reprocess existing audio until you run its action.

Batch Process Options (Main tab) — the steps Batch Process Dialogue runs
Neural cleanupOn by default, with GAP-URGENet. Natural (MossFormer) starts with MossFormer2. Untick to skip the model.
Level & compressOn by default. Levels each clip, compresses and lifts whispers, up to the Dialogue target. Turn off to hear the model on its own.
De-essOn by default and independent of Level & compress. Older presets and settings files keep it matched to their previous Level setting.
De-clickExperimental and off by default. Audition speech transients if enabled.
Extract ambience / music stemOff by default. Bandit runs only when requested, using the raw input independently of neural dialogue cleanup.
Mute raw when doneOn by default, below the menu. Untick to leave the originals audible after Batch Process Dialogue and Rebuild Speech.

Batch Process Dialogue is unavailable when no step is ticked. The menu is locked while an operation runs.

Clean dialogue (Options) — scope and clip handles
Source / selectionPer clip and Selected clips only are the default. Full sequence is a separate whole-mix workflow; use dialogue-only input.
Process in placeOn by default. Turn off to process in a cloned sequence.
Clip handlesOn by default for every preset. Requests 30 frames (in the sequence’s frame rate) of extra source per side for later trims/crossfades; change it in Frames per side (1–300). Source and host limitations may reduce or prevent handles; visible cuts stay in place.
Keep raw mixShare of the original clip blended back under the cleaned voice (0–50%). 0 by default; Promo/Action 15%, Camcorder 10%. Needs Neural cleanup.
Remove noiseNoise reduction (default 12 dB, 0–40) is how far matching noise is turned down. Noise threshold (default 6 dB, −6 to 24) is how far above the noise print a sound must be to be kept untouched: higher removes more but can thin quiet words. Saved with the preset.
Levels & stems — cleanup output targets

Default dialogue target: −12 dBFS. Default ambience target: −18 dBFS. Max level boost (default 8 dB, Camcorder 12 dB) limits how far Level & compress raises quiet dialogue. These cleanup/stem targets are not LUFS delivery targets. Speech level (default −23 dBFS, range −40 to −12) is the average level Level & compress brings each clip’s speech to before compressing peaks to the dialogue target; together they set how loud the dialogue ends up (roughly 10 dB below the dialogue target in LUFS). The YouTube preset uses Speech level −16 and dialogue −4 dBFS, which measured about −14 LUFS with true peaks near −2.6 dBTP on a raw interview clip. Its music −20 and ambience −10 targets apply to stems the service extracts (Stem separation); music clips already on the timeline are levelled by Level & duck music’s compressors instead, so check the full mix with Verify loudness and raise the music in the timeline if it sits too low.

Dialogue compressor (used by Level & compress; greyed out when it is off). The defaults are the settings the compressor has always used, so leaving them alone changes nothing.

Level speech before compressingOn by default. Brings each clip’s speech to Speech level first, so whispered and full-voice takes come out alike. Turn off to compress the clip as it is.
Compressor attackDefault 3 ms (0.5–50). How fast loud peaks are turned down. Slower lets more of each consonant through.
Compressor releaseDefault 60 ms (10–1000). How fast it lets go after a peak. Longer is smoother but can dip the start of the next word.
Compressor kneeDefault 12 dB (0–24). How far below the dialogue level compression starts, gently. 0 is a hard knee.
Max reductionDefault 24 dB (0–40). The most any moment can be turned down. A final ceiling still keeps peaks at the dialogue level.

The compressor works on two bands (150 Hz–4 kHz and above 4 kHz) after an 80 Hz high-pass; quiet passages can be lifted by up to Max level boost, the upper band by at most 2 dB. Every value is saved with the preset. These need the updated service (0.7.3).

Reset targets (this preset) resets cleanup and target settings only. Use the main preset reset for every preset option.

Ducking — reference and timing
Select music · name containsDefault ANW. The arrow button beside Level & duck music selects every audio clip whose clip or track name contains one of these words (comma-separated, capitals ignored). One setting for every preset; clearing it goes back to ANW.
Duck againstUse Clean dialogue, or Choose tracks and select the tracks containing your dialogue. Refresh updates that track list.
DuckDefault 10 dB: how far the selected music drops under speech.
Speech floorDefault −40 dBFS. Lower detects quieter speech; higher ignores more background sound.
Attack / releaseDefaults 200 ms / 1200 ms. Control how music moves down before speech and returns afterwards.
Music peak controlDefault −30 dB. Level & duck music adds Premiere’s Multiband Compressor “Broadcast” (−12 dB output gain, limiter bypassed) and then a second “Peak control” compressor that compresses music louder than this level.
Music peak ratioDefault 3:1 (1–30). How strongly Peak control compresses. Both are written into the native effects, so they stay editable in Effect Controls.
Remove clip VolumeOn by default. Removes native clip Volume and its keyframes before ducking. Turn off if you need to preserve existing Volume fades.

Level & duck music also applies native level-control compressors after ducking. Its Amplify keyframes and compressor effects remain editable in Effect Controls.

Loudness verification & dialogue quality

Loudness verification: Delivery fills in the target, tolerance and peak flag: Broadcast −24 LUFS ±1, peaks flagged over −6 dBFS; YouTube −14 LUFS ±1, peaks flagged over −2 dBFS. Editing any of those makes it Custom. You can also set the merge window and whether to add peak markers. Measures the full audible sequence.

About YouTube: YouTube publishes no official loudness spec, but its player plays everything at about −14 LUFS: louder uploads are turned down, quieter ones are not turned up. Aim for −14 LUFS with true peaks at or below −1 dBTP. The panel measures sample peaks, which read about 1–1.5 dB under true peaks, so its YouTube flag is −2 dBFS.

Dialogue quality: Flag below defaults to 1.5 (range 1–5). Lower is stricter about what gets flagged; higher sends more sections for review. Applies only to selected dialogue clips.

Panel menu and compact layout

The menu on the panel’s tab in Premiere offers Copy report, Open export folder, Change export folder, Connection settings, Save settings, Load settings, New preset from current settings, Compact panel, Don’t use cached files and Help: operator guide.

Help: operator guide opens this guide in your web browser. It is installed with the plugin, so it works offline and matches the installed version. The first time, Premiere may ask you to allow the plugin to open the file. If it cannot open it, the status line shows where the guide is (and copies that location) so you can open it yourself.

Don’t use cached files (off by default, remembered, applies to every preset) makes every run export, analyse and process again instead of reusing earlier exports, results or the service’s cache. The run log says when it is on.

Compact panel (off by default, remembered) hides the Copy report and Change buttons, hides Cancel until something is running, and hides the Connection row while connected; a small green dot beside the preset list shows the connection instead. Everything hidden stays available from the menu. Choose Compact panel again to bring the buttons back.

Presets, Save / Load and Reset

Each preset remembers its own valid settings. To make your own, choose + New preset… at the bottom of the preset list (or New preset from current settings… in the panel menu), type a name and click Save as new preset or press Enter: it stores the current choices under that name and switches to it. Delete this preset works only on custom presets and asks for a second click.

Save settings / Load settings writes or reads a reusable settings file. Connection credentials and sequence track choices are excluded. Named custom-preset lists stay local to the panel.

Reset this preset to defaults resets all of that preset’s processing/review fields, including dialogue quality. It does not reset other presets, the service URL, or duck-reference track picks. Changed from defaults lists your current differences.

06 / If something stops

Where are my files saved?

In a WTVAudioWizard folder inside the export folder shown at the top of the Main tab. A saved project uses its own folder unless you pick another with Change; the choice is remembered per project. Full sequence mode saves its results there too and no longer asks for a location. Keep the folder: the clips on your timeline link to those files.

Remove noise is disabled or says the noise print was not found

Remove noise needs a noise print for the open project: mark In and Out around noise only and click Learn noise. Prints are kept on the service that learned them, so after switching Service URL or reinstalling the service, learn noise again. Learn noise refuses a range shorter than 0.5 s or longer than 60 s, and one that renders as silence (a muted track or an empty part of the timeline).

A button is disabled or the service cannot be reached

Wait for the current operation to finish. For Batch Process Dialogue, Extract ambience, Rebuild Speech, Learn noise, Remove noise and Check dialogue quality, the service must be running and the panel connected: open Connection, check the Service URL (default http://192.168.0.176:8756, the office GPU server; no token) and click Connect. If Connect fails, ask whoever looks after the GPU server to check it is running (scripts/service-ctl.sh status). Select music, Level & duck music, Unmute, Select muted, Fix pop and Verify loudness work without a connection. Batch Process Dialogue also needs at least one step ticked in Batch Process Options. Check the status message for missing models or an interrupted batch. Level & duck music may remain unavailable while a cleanup batch is unfinished.

The result is too strong or sounds unnatural

Audition against the original without playing both at once. Use Unmute for originals the plugin silenced, and mute the new version for comparison. Adjust options before rerunning. Rebuild Speech re-creates the voice and can alter its character; keep the previous version when it sounds better.

An operation failed or I clicked Cancel

Read the status and inspect any audio already placed. Cancel does not undo completed work, and Premiere may finish an export already started. Copy report, note your selection and action, and include both when reporting an issue. If Batch Process Dialogue offers Resume, it can reuse verified progress.
